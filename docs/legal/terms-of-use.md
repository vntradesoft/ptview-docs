## 3. Data Storage, API Keys & Privacy Policy

- **Local-First Architecture:** Your chart workspaces, custom drawings, indicator templates, and AI API keys are stored locally on your machine under your user data directory (`%LocalAppData%\PTView` or the `data\` folder for Portable builds).
- **Exchange & AI Connectivity:** PTView connects directly from your device to public exchange APIs (OKX, Binance, Bybit) and to your configured AI provider endpoints. PTView never transmits your private AI API keys or trading workspaces to any third-party server.
- **Anonymous Crash & Error Telemetry (Enabled by Default; Opt-Out):** By default, anonymous crash stack traces and deduplicated background errors are sent to Sentry with IP addresses, machine identifiers, credentials, and user profile paths automatically scrubbed. You can disable this anytime under **Settings → General → Privacy & Diagnostics**.
- **Compliance & Consent Audit Trail:** When you accept this Agreement inside the application, the Software records a verifiable local consent audit entry (timestamp, policy version, document SHA-256 hash, and environment metadata) in your local configuration folder to verify regulatory and license compliance.
- **Privacy Inquiries:** For questions regarding data handling or our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md), please contact [support@ptview.app](mailto:support@ptview.app).
