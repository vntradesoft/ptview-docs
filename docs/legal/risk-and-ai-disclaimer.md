## 3. AI Copilot & MCP Tool Limitations

When using the built-in **AI Copilot** or external **MCP Server** integrations:

- **AI Hallucination & Analytical Errors:** Large Language Models (including Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Ollama, or custom providers) can hallucinate, misinterpret market structures, misread multi-timeframe context, or generate inaccurate support/resistance levels and indicator interpretations.
- **Human-in-the-Loop (No Automated Live Trading):** Order placement and live exchange trading tools are intentionally excluded from both the in-app AI Copilot and external MCP endpoints. You must independently verify all market prices, chart levels, and calculations directly on your exchange before executing any trade.
- **Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Connectivity:** Prompts and relevant chart context are transmitted directly from your device to the third-party AI provider you configure, subject to that provider's own terms and privacy policies.
