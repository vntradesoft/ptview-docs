# Trial & Refund Policy

:::warning Temporary Pro Pause & Trial Availability
Pro features, new 28-day Pro trials, and paid Pro upgrades are currently **temporarily locked while awaiting exchange commercial market-data licensing** (`OKX`, `Binance`, `Bybit`). **Community Edition** remains completely free for everyone. Once Pro sales resume, fresh installations will include trial evaluation before purchasing.
:::

---

## 1. Eligible Refund Cases (When Commercial Licensing Is Active)

We provide a **100% refund** under the following circumstances:

- **Duplicate Payments:** Accidental double transfers or duplicate payments for the same order.
- **Unresolved Activation Failure:** A critical technical failure where Pro entitlements cannot be unlocked on your workstation within **7 days** of purchase despite support assistance via Email or GitHub.

## 2. 1-Month & 1-Year Plan Scope (Beta)

- Beta Pro plans (**30 days** and **365 days**) cover **PtView Workstation** desktop usage and software updates on Windows.
- Third-party AI provider API costs (such as Gemini, OpenAI, or DeepSeek) are billed separately by your chosen AI provider and are not included.
- Neither plan auto-renews; upon expiration, the application automatically reverts to the free **Community Edition** while keeping your local workspaces intact.
- For the **1-Year plan**, available features and supported exchanges may change in accordance with each exchange's API terms and data policies.

## 3. How to Request a Refund or Billing Support

Email your order code (`PTV...`) and purchase email address to [support@ptview.app](mailto:support@ptview.app) for resolution within **24–48 business hours**.
