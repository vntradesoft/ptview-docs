# Drawing Tools & Chart Annotations

PTView includes a full suite of interactive drawing tools on the **left Drawing Toolbar** so you can mark trendlines, order blocks, Fibonacci levels, and trade notes directly on the chart.

![Drawing Toolbar and Chart Workspace](/img/screenshots/drawing-tools.png)

---

## 1. Toolbar Controls & Quick Helpers

Press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>D</kbd> anytime to show or hide the Drawing Toolbar. When you click any existing drawing on the chart, a **Floating Drawing Mini-Bar** pops up for 1-click color, thickness, line pattern, lock, or delete adjustments.

| Tool Icon / Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Crosshair** | Default cursor mode with synchronized price/time crosshair |
| **Trend & Lines** | Trend Line, Ray, Extended Line, Horizontal Line, Vertical Line, Trend Angle |
| **Fibonacci** | Fibonacci Retracement, Fibonacci Extension, Fibonacci Time Zones |
| **Zones & Shapes** | Rectangle Zone (Order Blocks / FVG), Ellipse, Triangle |
| **Text & Notes** | Text Annotation, Emoji Markers (`🚀`, `⚠️`, `🎯`), Arrow |
| **Price Measurement** | Measure price delta, % gain/loss, bar count, and elapsed time |
| **Magnet Mode** | Automatically snaps endpoints to the nearest candle High, Low, Open, or Close |
| **Lock All Drawings** | Prevents accidental dragging while panning or zooming charts |

---

## 2. Common Trader Workflows

### Mark Support / Resistance Zones
1. Select **Rectangle Zone** (or **Horizontal Line**) from the left Drawing Toolbar.
2. Enable **Magnet Mode** (horseshoe magnet icon) so the box edge snaps cleanly to candle wicks.
3. Drag across the price range, then click the zone to customize fill color and opacity in the floating toolbar.

### Measure Risk / Reward & Price Moves
1. Select the **Measurement (Ruler)** tool.
2. Click at your entry candle and drag to your target or stop level — PTView displays the exact dollar move, **% change**, **number of bars**, and **elapsed duration**.

### Manage All Drawings (`Ctrl+Shift+D`)
- **Select & Move**: Click any object to reveal resize handles; drag the middle handle to move.
- **Clone**: Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> while dragging an object to duplicate it.
- **Delete**: Press <kbd>Delete</kbd> on a selected object, or open **Drawing Manager** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>D</kbd>) to filter, toggle visibility, lock, or delete multiple drawings across panes.

:::tip Draw with AI Copilot
You can also ask **AI Copilot** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>J</kbd>) to draw for you, e.g. *"Highlight the 64,200–64,600 support zone with a green box and mark the recent swing high with a Fibonacci Retracement."*
:::
